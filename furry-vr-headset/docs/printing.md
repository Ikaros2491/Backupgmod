# Printing guide

## Recommended settings

| Part | Material | Layer | Infill | Supports |
|------|----------|------:|-------:|----------|
| Shell outer (split) | PETG | 0.28 | 15–20% gyroid | Yes for snout overhangs |
| Optical chassis / carriers | PETG | 0.20 | 30% | Minimal |
| Face interface | PETG | 0.16 | 25% | No |
| Cooling ducts | PETG | 0.20 | 20% | As needed |
| Camera cradles | PETG | 0.16 | 40% | No |

- Use **heat-set inserts** for all M3 joints that are serviced more than once.  
- Orient IPD rail holes horizontally if possible for rounder bores; ream with a 3.2 mm drill.  
- For the shell, split along the sagittal plane or snout/cranial cut in your slicer if the bed is under 300 mm.

## Post-process

1. Tap / insert all bosses before first assembly.  
2. Line duct joints with foam tape for air sealing.  
3. Paint or epoxy-coat the outer shell before fur if you want a smoother glue surface.  
4. Do **not** cover cheek intake mesh zones or crown exhaust with dense foam.
